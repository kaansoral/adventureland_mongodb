The Tenth Anniversary

During the anniversary, a non-AFK player is picked every 30 minutes on each non-PvP realm. Open the anniversary button to find them. Reach them within five minutes and use I Kiss You from within 80 range. You do not need to own the emote.

Each character's first valid kiss that round gives one cake slice and one Anniversary Gift. Repeated kisses give no extra rewards. Your own account's characters cannot earn rewards by kissing each other. The featured player receives one Gift after their first eligible visitor.

Every character on your account finds the same slice flavor, on every realm. Slices are tradeable: collect all six flavors, then bring one of each and 100,000 gold to Mira in Town. Her workshop also lets you choose a particular reward using a cake and the listed ingredients.

Opening a Sixfold Cake gives one anniversary equipment item and three Gifts, with a separate, extremely rare chance of an I Kiss You CX Jar. Gifts mainly contain gold, with chances for returning anniversary prizes, equipment, or emote jars. Make a Wish costs 50 MP and has a four-minute cooldown. Both jar unlocks are permanent; the permanent kiss remains usable on other players after the event.

Ordinary eligible monsters also have separate slice and Gift rolls. Luck does not increase these two seasonal rolls. Mira leaves when the event ends, but unopened cakes and Gifts remain usable.

Check server.status.anniversary. Its live field identifies an active visit round; target, id, map, x, and y identify the featured player. expires and next are timestamps in milliseconds. Travel normally, then use use_skill("ikissyou", server.status.anniversary.id) when nearby.
Home and Blessings

Speak with Bean while on the realm this character wants to call home. At home, your contribution against cooperative monsters grows five times faster before rewards are shared. Selected monsters can also carry extra home rewards.

If another non-merchant character on your account visited a different server during the previous 30 minutes, you receive 30 minutes of Realm Fatigue when you enter. You can still fight and receive normal rewards, but the home contribution bonus and home-only drops wait until the condition ends. Switching again renews the condition. Merchants are ignored.

Realm Fatigue limits quick event hopping without changing any character's home. Stay on one server for 30 minutes and its home rewards return.

Changing home has a 36-hour cooldown, so read Bean's confirmation instead of using it as ordinary travel.

Favoré's blessing is realm-wide and spends Shells. Her panel shows the exact current cost and remaining blessing time. This is separate from setting a home server.
