THE PLAYER ECONOMY
Markets & Trading

Merchant stands let players list items for sale, publish buy orders, and hold giveaways. Prices come from adventurers, not NPCs.

Manual

Merchants can buy a stand from Divian. Open it, place an item in a trade slot, set its quantity and price, then leave the stand open. A wishlist reverses the trade: you offer gold for a named item and level.

To trade with another player, stand nearby and open their merchant panel. Re-read the item, level, quantity, and total before confirming. Giveaways are timed stand listings that eligible players may join.

Ponty sells equipment recovered through the ordinary second-hand market. Ron in the West Office keeps unlooted-chest finds; a qualifying donation to the gold reserve is required before he shows that stock.

CODE
async function market_example() { await open_stand(); await trade(0, 1, 100000, 1); // inventory 0 -> trade slot 1 await wishlist(2, "staff", 10000000, 9, 1); var merchant = get_player("MerchantName"); if (merchant) await trade_buy(merchant, "trade1", 1); // When finished: await close_stand(); }

References: open_stand, close_stand, trade, wishlist, trade_buy, trade_sell, giveaway, join_giveaway.

Read the nearby player's current trade slots before buying or selling. Trade calls include the listing ID, so a changed offer is refused.
