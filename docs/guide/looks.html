Cosmetics

Cosmetics change how your character looks. They do not take an equipment slot.

Open the Wardrobe

Click your character, then COSMETICS. Pick a slot such as Hair, Hat, Face, Attire, Back, Special, or RIP. Owned choices appear first; gray choices show what is still out there.

Click an owned look to equip it. Choose the red empty tile to clear that slot. The complete gallery is under GUIDE → All Cosmetics.

Finding Looks

Looks can come from cosmetic shops, exchanges, monsters, achievements, classes, equipment, events, and Cosmetic Jars. The gallery marks exchange, monster, class, and item sources with small letters.

Some looks have limited copies. Equipping one does not consume it. Sending one to another character does.

Sharing Between Characters

Open the Cosmetics panel of another character on your account. Choose a look you own to send one copy to that character. Cosmetics cannot be sent to a different account.

With CODE

equip_cx equips or clears a cosmetic slot. send_cx moves one owned copy to another character on your account.

show_json(character.cx); // currently equipped show_json(character.acx); // owned copies await equip_cx("hat", "bwhat"); await equip_cx("hat"); // clear the hat slot await send_cx("YourOtherCharacter", "bwhat");
