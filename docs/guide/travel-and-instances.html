Four Ways to Move
smart_move()Plans and follows a collision-aware multi-map route to a place, NPC, monster, event, or coordinates.
transport()Uses a nearby transporter route point. It does not walk you to Alia first.
use_nearest_door()Uses a door already within local range. It rejects instead of walking to a distant door.
town()Channels a return to the map's town position. Movement, damage, or another interruption can stop it.
Wait for one travel Promise before starting another. A different smart_move() destination interrupts the active route; repeating the same destination shares it.

CRYPTSame Private Map

Stand at the matching entrance. One party member uses the required key to open a named instance; the others enter with exactly that same name while the instance is available. A different name creates or targets a different place.

Instances do not remain alive forever when nobody is inside and online. Coordinate the name before entering and keep at least one party member present while others travel.
