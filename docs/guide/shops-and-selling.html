THE SIMPLE ECONOMY
Shops & Selling

NPC merchants sell dependable supplies and buy unwanted items. Player merchants and buy orders belong to the wider market.

Manual

Stand near a merchant and open their shop. Inspect an item, then right-click it to buy. With a merchant interface open, right-click an inventory item to sell it. Locked items cannot be sold.

Ponty deals in second-hand equipment. His stock changes as adventurers sell things back into the world.

CODE

Without a Computer, stand near a merchant who carries the item. Use the explicit currency routine when your intent matters.

async function restock() { await buy("hpot0", 100); await buy_with_gold("mpot0", 100); // Sell one item from inventory slot 0. await sell(0, 1); }

References: buy, buy_with_gold, buy_with_shells, sell.

Check the item and quantity immediately before selling. CODE sales do not ask for a confirmation.
For player shops, continue to Markets and Trading.
