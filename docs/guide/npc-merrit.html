Merrit

Keep a shop on Mainland's square. Merrit brings Market Parcels to traders who leave room for their neighbors.

Set up shopAny class can take part. Open a stand with an item for sale or a buy order funded for at least one unit. Giveaways alone don't count.
Stay two minutesKeep your stock and your spot for two continuous minutes. Your account can receive one parcel per hour, across all characters and servers.

She must walk within 32px with a clear path to deliver. Following her and opening a stand won't skip the two-minute wait.

Market Parcels

Each visit gives 1 Market Parcel. Trade it, bank it, or exchange one with Xyn for supplies or a rare find. Computer exchange access also works.

1 in 900 parcels holds one of five rare T3 items. Each is 1 in 4,500. Click an item to inspect it.

All rewards and chances

Exchange 1 parcel for 1 result. Luck, stand type and sales volume do not change these chances.

Leave room

No parcels within 40px of a stationary NPC. Keep the entrances to Ponty, Favoré and other fixed NPCs clear. Roaming citizens don't count when they pause.

No parcels within 10px of another open stand. At 10px or closer, both shops are blocked.

No parcels directly in front of another stand: up to 15px south and within 10px sideways. Only the front shop is blocked by this rule.

Distances use character foot positions. Trading still works in these spaces.

Your shop
Where she walks

Merrit stays within 600px of (0,0). Your stand must be in the outlined square or southern aisle, on pavement she can reach.

White dot: (0,0). Square: x −240 to 240, y −120 to 144. Southern aisle: x −88 to 88, y 144 to 360. Water, blocked ground and the spacing exclusions do not qualify.

Timing, stock and bag space

Moving, closing or reopening, disconnecting, leaving the area, losing your last qualifying listing, or failing the spacing rules restarts the two-minute settling time. Replacing listings while another valid listing remains keeps your progress.

After each delivery, settle for two minutes again. The one-hour account cooldown runs at the same time. Time away never builds up parcels. Ready shops get priority, but delivery still waits for Merrit's visit.

Parcels stack up to 9,999. Keep an unlocked matching stack with room, or one free slot. A full bag doesn't consume a reward or restart the account cooldown. Exchanges need space for their result.

An occasional extra: 1 SHELL

Sometimes Merrit adds 1 SHELL directly to your account. Below 10 SHELLS, the chance is higher and falls as your balance approaches 10.

SHELLS before the visitChance of +1 SHELL

The chance is 0.5% at zero SHELLS and 0.001% at 10 or more. Spending SHELLS can raise it again. Buying, trading or exchanging parcels gives no extra visit-bonus roll.

A sparkle and a coin sound mark each delivery. Click Merrit to talk or recall her last gift.
