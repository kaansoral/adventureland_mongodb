White dot: (0,0). Square: x −240 to 240, y −120 to 144. Southern aisle: x −88 to 88, y 144 to 360. Water, blocked ground and the spacing exclusions do not qualify.

The hour, your stock, and delivery
  • Keep a stocked stand in one place for 60 continuous minutes before your first parcel, and again after each delivery. Giveaways alone don't count. Buy orders must be funded for at least one unit.
  • Moving, closing or reopening, disconnecting, leaving the area, losing your last qualifying listing, or failing the spacing rules restarts the hour. Replacing listings while another valid listing remains keeps your progress.
  • Merrit visits ready shops first. She must come within 32px with a clear path. Becoming eligible after an hour doesn't promise immediate delivery. Following her and opening a stand won't shorten the wait.
  • Your account can receive one parcel per hour across all characters and servers. Time away never builds up parcels.
  • Parcels stack up to 9,999. Keep an unlocked matching stack with room, or one free slot. A full bag doesn't consume your hour. You can trade or bank parcels; exchanging them needs room for the result.
