bank_store(num, pack, pack_num)

Moves an inventory item into a bank pack, automatically selecting a stack or free bank slot when no pack is supplied.

Returns: Promise resolving with the bank response, or rejecting for conditions such as not_in_bank, no_item, or bank_full.

Promise failures: Unless a function-specific note says otherwise, failures reject with an object containing failed: true and a reason. After mode_resolve_all(), the same failures fulfill instead.

Example:

bank_store(0, "items0", 0);
