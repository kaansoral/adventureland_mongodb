stop_character(name)

Stops another character from your account that was started with start_character().

Returns: Nothing; the runner stop is requested immediately.

Mainframe: Stopping one included worker leaves its root and other workers running. The change affects the next renewal only; it does not reprice time already paid for. Disconnecting the root character from Mainframe stops every included character and disables the group's future renewal.

Example:

stop_character("Merchant");
